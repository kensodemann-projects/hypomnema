# Hypomnema

A hypomnema is a note kept so it can be found again: a reminder, a memorandum, an excerpt, or a short record of something worth remembering. In the plural, hypomnemata were the notebooks kept for that purpose, pages of quotations, observations, and things heard or read, gathered so they would still be there later.

Hypomnema is a personal application to help you meet daily, weekly, and monthly goals, such as "meditate daily," "exercise four times per week," or "lose twenty pounds by the end of the year." You record what you do, see how you are tracking against each goal, and keep useful resources alongside it. A journal, with its own journaling goal, gives you a place to write and to watch your mood over time.

## Accounts

Hypomnema is invitation only. Accounts are created for you, and there is no sign-up. Sign in with your email and password. If you forget your password, request a reset email from the sign-in screen.

Your data is private. Nobody else using Hypomnema can see your goals, entries, or journal.

## Goals

A goal can be one of four types:

- **Frequency** — Do something a number of times per day, week, or month (e.g., "exercise daily"). Log each time you do it.
- **Accumulation** — Reach a total per day, week, or month (e.g., "exercise 30 minutes daily"). Log an amount each time, such as 45 minutes.
- **Target value** — Reach a value by a date (e.g., "weigh 180 lb by December 31"). Log your current value as you go.
- **Avoidance** — Stop doing something, either for good or for a set number of days (e.g., "no desserts in December"). Your streak counts up on its own, and you record only a slip. When you slip on a goal with a set number of days, you choose what happens: start the count over, mark the goal missed but keep going until its original end date, or mark it missed and end it.

Weeks run Sunday through Saturday. A goal can start today or on a future date. An avoidance goal can also start in the past, so it reflects a streak you already have.

You can rename a goal, update its description, or change its target at any time. A new target applies from the current period forward. A goal's type and period are fixed once the goal starts. Until then, you can change anything about it.

When you no longer want to track a goal, you can **archive** it to keep its history, or **delete** it to remove it completely. Archiving cannot be undone.

## Tracking

Track everything from the dashboard. Each goal has a panel showing how you are doing this period, with a quick way to log:

- **Done** for a frequency goal
- **An amount or value** for accumulation and target value goals
- **"Did you slip today?"** for an avoidance goal

Each entry can have a short memo, such as "Boxing" or "Evening walk." If you forgot to log something, you can date an entry up to 7 days back. You can edit or delete an entry within 24 hours of creating it.

Select a goal's panel to see its history, all of its entries, and its resources.

## Resources

Each goal can hold **resources**: links, routines, articles, or anything else that helps you with that goal. A resource has a subject, an optional link, and an optional description.

## Journal

Your journal has a journaling goal, once per day by default. You can change it to any number of entries per day, week, or month.

Each journal entry has:

- **Subject** — Optional.
- **Mood** — From 1 ("Awful") to 5 ("Great"), shown as a colored number.
- **Journal** — Your writing.

Entries are dated automatically when you save them. You can edit an entry within 24 hours of creating it. If you're not sure what to write, select **I'm Stuck** for a random prompt. Select it again for a different one.

Journal entries can't be deleted, but you can make one **private**. A private entry shows only its date, time, and mood. **Show Content** reveals the rest while you have the entry open, and it is hidden again when you leave. To make the entry visible again permanently, make it public, which you can do at any time.

The journal also shows a mood trend over time.

## Platforms

Hypomnema runs on Android phones and on macOS desktops. Each has its own layout. Your data syncs between devices.
